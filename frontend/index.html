HOW TO PLAY

Escape the dungeon

Your objective

Find the treasure chest, pick it up, then return to the blue portal. Returning without treasure is only a safe escape, not a treasure win.

Move

Use arrow keys, WASD, swipe on the board, or the touch controls. A move into a wall does not advance you.

Dragon

The dragon sleeps until you get close. Once awake it hunts every turn. If you reach the portal carrying treasure, the portal wins even if the dragon is on it.

Hearts

You begin with 3 hearts. If the dragon catches you, one heart carries you safely back to the portal and your level is kept. When you have fewer than 3, a heart has a 10% chance to appear at the start of a run exactly three tiles from the dragon. Reaching it wakes the dragon. Bring it back to the portal to bank it for the next run; it cannot save the current run. A true defeat with no hearts restores all 3 at Level 1.

Difficulty

Normal: the dragon learns walls as it hunts. Hard: it knows every wall from the start.

Levels

Levels 1–6 grow from 8×8 to 13×13. Level 7 adds randomized walls and dead ends. At Level 10 the dragon gains Hard instincts. Wall density reaches its tested safe maximum at Level 54.

Rankings

Treasure wins, best move count, and streak are stored on the server. A dragon loss resets your next run to Level 1.
